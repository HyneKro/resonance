Privacy & platform details ↗
Is this a streaming service?

No. Resonance is a player for your local music collection. You supply the songs; no subscription or Resonance account is required. Once files are on your device, local playback works offline.

Does it download Spotify or Deezer audio?

No. Public Spotify and Deezer playlists provide titles, artists and order. Resonance helps you review matching YouTube recordings and downloads audio from the selected match. Availability varies; use music you’re allowed to download.

Will updates keep my library?

Yes. Use the in-app update flow: Restart & update on Windows, or Install update on Android. Keep the Windows data folder and don’t uninstall the Android app to update it.

Does my music get uploaded?

Local playback and sound analysis stay on your device. Optional lyrics, artwork and metadata searches send song or artist search terms to external services. Update checks contact GitHub. See what goes online →

Can I sync my PC and phone?

There is no automatic PC-to-phone library sync yet. Each installation manages its own files, playlists and settings. Windows and Android also have independent version numbers.
