# Resonance 1.7.0 — Explore your constellation

Constellation gives your library more room: stars are spaced farther apart, genre and artist labels smoothly frame their groups, and eight star forms add variety. The sharper Milky Way softens when a song is selected. Every song sharing a credited artist is highlighted, with stronger connections nearby.

- A persistent Now playing card shows the current song, artist and genre while you explore; its Details panel follows track changes.
- The main mode button becomes Visualizer inside Constellation. Removed the duplicate library entry, bookmarks and the all-songs panel. Search and Surprise me help find a starting point.
- The normal visualizer releases its graphics resources and stops audio analysis while the map is active. Playback continues.
- Genre discovery recognizes more source labels and release suffixes, searches alternate upload identities with duration checks, and uses verified local artist catalogues for explicitly marked suggestions. Remixes are researched separately. Results stay local and manual genre edits remain protected; unsupported genres remain unknown.
- The PC queue is one scrolling timeline: listening history, the current song, then editable upcoming tracks. It opens around the playing song and preserves your scroll position when edited.

Update in place to keep your music, playlists and settings. This release rearranges the constellation once for the expanded spacing, then keeps those positions stable.
