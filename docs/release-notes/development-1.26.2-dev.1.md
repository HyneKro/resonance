# Synapse — living connections

Windows **1.26.2-dev.1** and Android **0.26.2-dev.1** (versionCode **107**), isolated development channel.

Synapse keeps its airy polygon network, fine woven fans, translucent facets, soft depth and music colours. Its reaction is rebuilt around small currents travelling through actual connections.

- Large expanding shockwaves, radial pressure deformation and outward particle explosions are removed. Percussion now chooses a frequency-related junction and sends a small luminous signal through neighbouring links. It visits several junctions, gradually loses energy and leaves a short bright trail. Signals follow the moving web in 3D.
- Bass produces a restrained elastic flex. Harmonic bands shape local motion and flowing highlights inside the woven fibres. Texture adds slow detail. Attack/release smoothing, a longer refractory period and bounded local charge keep busy percussion readable without flashing the entire network.
- Junctions brighten locally as a signal approaches, then settle. The musical-flashes control adjusts those local highlights. Bloom is concentrated around active points and trails; its global modulation and the background's brightness variation are reduced.
- The underlying web moves more slowly and reconnects through crossfades. Existing currents, colour and geometry continue across song changes; pausing lets signals finish and activity settle into slow idle movement. Reduced-motion and explicit hold behaviour remain respected.
- Desktop uses 1,700 junctions and at most 36 simultaneous routed signals. Phone rendering stays at 680 junctions and 16 routes. The former dense all-link transient trail pass is replaced with a small bounded current pass.

Verification: all **562 shared tests** passed, including deterministic nearest-neighbour routing, continuity at junctions, sustained percussion staying local, bounded motion, pause decay, band-specific response, track continuity and frame-rate stability. Cross-scene GPU checks passed for all seven visual worlds, including the spectrum-illumination and musical-flashes controls, context recovery and phone budgets. Focused GPU checks cover desktop/phone layouts, warm/cool palettes, several camera angles, local current emission, bloom, facets, hold/reduced motion and smooth RGBA8 background gradients. A short generated-audio preview is available locally; it has no sound or personal library data. Automated phone layouts and timings are desktop simulations, not physical Android testing.

Both development artifacts require the full shared suite, Android build/JVM/lint checks, extracted startup module graphs, clean Windows launch, signing, legacy-updater compatibility, uploaded hashes and anonymous update-channel verification before publication.
