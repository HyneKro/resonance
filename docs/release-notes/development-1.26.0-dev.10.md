# Flux: a wave with depth

Windows **1.26.0-dev.10** · Android **0.26.0-dev.10** (versionCode 76). Development channel only.

Open **Visuals → Style → Art direction → Flux**. The wave now rotates around the centre of its history, with fine filaments that remain visible edge-on, crosswise threads and a clearer undercurrent. Older spectral trails retain enough light to read from behind. Particle cores, small pixel glints, depth softness and restrained glow refine the surface. Sparse drifting motes and subtle camera-aware haze add depth around the wave while preserving open space.

Drag to inspect all sides, or turn on the camera rail. **Orbiting dust** controls the surrounding motes; set it to zero for a cleaner composition. Sculpture scale, particle size, glow, bloom and colour modes remain available. Camera zoom is gentler for Flux to keep the full volume in view. The existing bass, melody, percussion and frequency response remains based on the same 48-band analysis and approximately four seconds of history. Paused/frozen playback holds the scene, a new song clears old history, and silence drains the wave. The sparse atmosphere can remain in silence.

Both runtimes share the renderer. Android keeps fewer traces, particles and surrounding motes, adaptive resolution and its existing portrait composition. No image assets, new analysis models or network requests were added.

Development installations receive this build through **Settings → App updates**. An ordinary installation needs a one-time development ZIP/APK installation to enter this channel. Keep Windows testing in a separate portable folder. Android installs over the existing signed app and preserves data; confirm through the system installer and do not uninstall. The main branch, ordinary releases and public download buttons remain unchanged.
