# Resonance Android 0.7.0-preview — Explore your constellation

- More room between stars, smooth genre/artist group zoom, eight star forms, a sharper sky and stronger same-artist highlights.
- A compact Now playing card stays visible while exploring. Open Details for the song's genre, artist signature and connections; it follows playback changes.
- The map's mode button becomes Visualizer. Removed the duplicate library entry, bookmarks and all-songs panel; search and Surprise me remain.
- Normal visualizer rendering and analysis stop while Constellation is active, without interrupting music.
- Improved genre labels, upload-title matching and locally saved artist catalogue suggestions. Manual edits remain protected; suggestions carry ≈ and unverified songs stay unknown.

ARM64 preview, including Galaxy S22+. Install over your current app; do not uninstall. Music, playlists, settings and the signing identity are preserved. Constellation positions are rearranged once for the expanded spacing.

Validated with automated Android tests, lint, package checks and phone-size interface previews. This release has not been tested on a physical S22+ here.
