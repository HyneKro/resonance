# Constellation — explore your music and build a journey

Open **Library → Constellation** to explore a stable star map of your local songs. Search, pan and zoom; select a star for playback controls and related songs with reasons for the connection. A song list provides an alternative to the canvas.

Build a journey from a starting song toward a destination or mood. Choose its approximate duration, favour familiar songs or discoveries, and pin up to five stops. Review, reorder, replace or remove songs before playing; save the result as a normal playlist. Play after current preserves your position and replaces the upcoming queue. Play now starts immediately.

Map positions and bookmarks stay on your computer. Saved audio measurements and genre clues guide the map; unmeasured songs are labelled clearly. Optional sound-data preparation runs one file at a time. Refine map explicitly applies the newest data to star positions. No music is uploaded.

Includes the previous Android artwork/analysis repairs in the corresponding phone release. Existing music, settings and playlists are preserved.

Validation: shared model, local API, queue and 2,500-song regression checks; desktop and phone-width browser interaction checks; extracted-package startup asset checks. Android native queue instrumentation is compiled, but this release has not been exercised on a connected physical phone.
