# Constellation for Android

Open **Library → Constellation** to explore your music as a star map. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, or search and use Song list. Select a star for its cover, playback controls and related songs.

Build and review journeys using a starting song, a destination or mood, an approximate duration, Familiar ↔ Explore and up to five pinned stops. Reorder, replace or remove songs; save journeys as regular playlists. Play after current preserves playback position; Play now starts the journey. Explicit queue actions turn shuffle off to honour the chosen order.

Positions, bookmarks and sound measurements stay on your phone. Bulk sound-data preparation requires a charger and processes one local song at a time while the view is open. The map redraws on interaction and visible playback updates rather than running a continuous animation loop.

Install over your existing app to preserve music, settings and playlists. ARM64 preview, retaining the existing package and signing identity. Includes the preceding artwork and audio-analysis fixes.

Validation: shared model/API checks, Android JVM tests, compiled native queue instrumentation, lint, extracted APK assets and a phone-width browser bridge fixture. Physical-device playback and touch behaviour still need testing on a phone.
