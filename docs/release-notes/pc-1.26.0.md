# Resonance 1.26.0 — new visual worlds

The approved visualizer and playlist improvements are now available through the ordinary update channel: **Windows 1.26.0** and **Android 0.26.0-preview** (versionCode **104**).

- **Astra** surrounds a detailed, animated star with frequency-driven orbital waves, luminous eruptions, orbiting particles and gravitational comet trails. Stronger musical colour changes, bloom and smooth scene motion keep it expressive through playback changes.
- **Contour** paints an animated ink waveform on bright paper, with falling droplets, drifting pigment, pond-like ripples and soft moving leaf shadows. Its painting carries across songs and settles gently on pause.
- **Synapse** forms a freely evolving geometric web with nearby connections, translucent facets, travelling light waves, particles and strong musical deformation.
- **Flux** turns frequency history into a glowing particle waveform against a black background, with longer connected echoes, subtle digital motes and free camera exploration. Scroll continues to control volume.
- **Afterglow** drives through a neon synthwave landscape, with layered city silhouettes, changing musical windows, shaded palms, mirrored terrain echoes, pixel particles and adjustable CRT texture. Road markings follow the road perspective.
- **Aerial** combines chrome, marble, gold, glass and emissive spheres in a dark studio. Smoothed currents, surface deformation, bubbles and restrained bass/loudness-driven physics add detail and motion.
- The original **Resonance** world remains available. Visuals settings can stay open while rotating the scene; new worlds use smoother pause/play and song transitions, with mobile detail defaults preserved.
- Playlist setup explains public access and Spotify’s **Make public** option. Spotify/Deezer provide song lists; choose a YouTube recording before downloading its audio. Unmatched songs now open recording search from the selection box, and Android preserves chosen recordings after playlist rechecks.

Use **Settings → App updates** on ordinary installations. Windows installs after your restart action; Android uses the system installer and updates the existing app. Do not uninstall Android. Music, playlists, settings and signing identity are preserved.

Development installations remain on their separate channel. To move to this approved release, install the Windows ZIP through **Install a downloaded ZIP…**, or install the Android APK over the existing app. Android versionCode 104 is newer than dev.37. Source-folder Windows runs use the updated source.

Validation covers the shared automated suite, Android JVM tests and lint, isolated desktop/phone rendering and playlist flows, both packaged startup module graphs, clean empty-library Windows startup, Android package/signature/version checks, legacy Windows updater compatibility and uploaded artifact hashes. Physical Android device testing has not been performed. Private playlist sign-in is not added; Spotify’s anonymous playlist page may expose only its first 100 tracks.
