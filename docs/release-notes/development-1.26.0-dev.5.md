# Contour: a living ink painting

Windows **1.26.0-dev.5** · Android **0.26.0-dev.5** (versionCode 71). Development channel only.

Open **Visuals → Style → Art direction → Contour**. Resonance remains the default.

Contour now has its own fully flat ink-and-paper composition. An open circle, a flowing S stroke and a softened diamond alternate through drawing and drying phases. A loaded ink wash, irregular bristles, tapered ends and paper grain replace the nearly static intersecting circles. Quiet typography, a geometric red seal and a small live frequency strip frame the painting.

Bass opens and bends the geometry, melody sends distortions along the brush, and percussion changes its pressure. Rising audible accents deposit vermilion splashes that spread, settle and dry. A sustained loud note does not continually spray particles. Frequency lights alter pigment density rather than adding a glowing 3D material.

The painting has no camera perspective or lighting. Its lettering remains upright on phones, with a vertical composition and reduced brush complexity. Contour keeps its print layout when a kaleidoscope is selected; other directions retain their existing behavior. Pause holds the painting timeline, reduced motion suppresses deformation and new splashes, and switching away does no hidden simulation or catch-up. A new song clears old marks.

The visual review iterated on wire-like marks, overlapping typography, folds under strong deformation, red saturation and portrait clipping. This is a procedural interpretation of brushwork, not a fluid simulation. The requested 9.8/10 remains an artistic target, not an independently established result; please compare quiet acoustic music, voices and dense percussion during the trial.
