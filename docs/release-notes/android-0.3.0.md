A clearer way around Resonance, with your music and preferences preserved.

- Six focused Settings categories replace the long mixed list. Playback, audio sync and interface feedback have their own tabs; Visuals & Sona is grouped into Style, Motion, Display and Extras.
- Behind the sound separates Overview, Lyrics, Listening edit and Edit details. Primary song actions stay within reach, and lyrics no longer trap the outer menu scroll.
- Back follows the route you actually took and keeps your place, selected tab and unfinished form. Closing a panel returns focus to its opener.
- Clearer Add music choices distinguish existing files from online playlist downloads. Help & gestures makes controls easy to find.

- The phone library keeps All songs, Favorites and Downloaded visible, with collections in a selector and sorting/filtering in a compact disclosure.
- Queue has separate Up next and History tabs. Song details use a compact cover header and accessible playback actions.
- More legible translucent sheets reduce text showing through from the player. Exact saved slider values remain consistent with their labels.

ARM64 preview, versionCode 19. Install over the existing app to retain music, settings and playlists; do not uninstall first. Native playback and downloader behavior are unchanged.

Validation: build, 74 JVM tests, Android lint (0 errors, 17 warnings), extracted APK startup dependency checks, and phone-width menu flows with a simulated native bridge. No physical Android device was available for this release.
