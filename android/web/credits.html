Music information

MusicBrainz, the Apple catalogue and LRCLIB provide optional metadata, artwork and lyrics lookups. Results are cached on this phone. Titles and artist names are sent to these services when looking up a song; audio files are not uploaded. Source labels identify genre suggestions.

Artwork and lyrics belong to their respective creators. The app contains no bundled music or personal library.

Constellation sky

Official builds incorporate a reduced panorama from Space Skybox Collection 4 by Makemake, licensed through Fab for use within Resonance. The purchased artwork is not covered by the app source licence or available for standalone redistribution. Public source builds include a procedural fallback.
